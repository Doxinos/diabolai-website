Questions
1) How fast can we start?
Most businesses go live in days. We begin with a simple call flow and expand.

2) What if a caller asks something complex?
The agent gathers details and escalates to your team automatically.

Yes. We integrate with popular CRMs and schedulers — or start with email summaries and add integrations later.

We minimize PHI/PII in prompts, control access to logs, and tailor to your compliance needs.

5) What languages/voices are available?
Multiple voices and languages are available; we’ll match your brand tone.

6) How is pricing structured?
Simple tiers by usage and features. See /pricing.

7) Can I review conversations and coach the AI?
Yes. You’ll get summaries, logs, and a feedback loop to improve.

8) When should a human step in?
We route edge cases and escalations based on rules you approve.
