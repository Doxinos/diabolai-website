### 3. FRAMEWORK PAGE (/framework)

**Status:** 🆕 NEW PAGE - Phase 2

**Purpose:** Deep dive into Diagnose → Redesign → Automate methodology

**Structure:**

#### 3.1 Hero
- H1: "The Diagnose → Redesign → Automate Framework"
- Subtitle: "Our proven 3-phase approach to AI transformation that ensures you solve real problems, not just automate bad processes"

#### 3.2 Why This Framework Works
**Problem:** Most AI projects fail because they automate without diagnosing or redesigning first.

**Our Approach:**
- Start with diagnosis (not tools)
- Redesign processes before automating
- Choose minimal effective technology
- Measure results continuously

#### 3.3 Phase Breakdown (Interactive/Visual)

**Phase 1: Diagnose (1-2 weeks)**
- Activities: Audit operations, map data flows, identify bottlenecks, prioritize opportunities
- Questions we ask: Where are you bleeding time/money? What processes follow patterns? Where is human judgment critical vs routine?
- Output: Opportunity matrix with ROI estimates
- Investment: $3K-$10K (AI Diagnosis)

**Phase 2: Redesign (1-2 weeks)**
- Activities: Process mapping, workflow optimization, KPI definition, risk assessment
- Questions we ask: What would this look like if designed for AI? What can we eliminate? What needs human oversight?
- Output: Redesigned process maps + success metrics
- Investment: $10K-$25K (Roadmap Sprint)

**Phase 3: Automate (4-12 weeks)**
- Activities: Tech selection, solution build, integration, testing, deployment
- Tech options: AI agents, Voice AI, RPA, no-code tools, custom solutions
- Output: Live automation + monitoring + documentation
- Investment: $25K-$75K (Implementation)

#### 3.4 Case Study Integration
**Show 2-3 examples of the framework in action**

**Example 1: Professional Services Firm**
- Diagnose: Found slow quote process causing lost deals
- Redesign: Simplified discovery questions, created templates
- Automate: 20% automation of quote generation
- Result: 70% faster quotes, +30% close rate

**Example 2: Manufacturing Company**
- Diagnose: Inventory chaos → over-ordering, cash flow issues
- Redesign: New ordering rules based on actual consumption
- Automate: Light automation for reorder triggers
- Result: $100K/year savings, stable inventory
