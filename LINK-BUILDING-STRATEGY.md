# Link Building & Citation Strategy for Diabol AI

**Goal:** Increase AI visibility from 2.8/10 to 7.0/10 by building trust node coverage and high-quality backlinks

**Based on:** AI Citation Audit (Nov 16, 2025) - 28% trust node coverage, 0% AI citation rate

---

## Strategy Overview

The audit revealed Diabol AI is virtually invisible to AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) because of:
1. Missing trust nodes (only 8/29 found)
2. Weak cross-link signals (4.5/10)
3. Zero presence on aggregator sites that LLMs cite

**The Fix:** Systematic link building targeting the exact sources AI systems use when answering "top AI consulting firms" queries.
